# Test vehicle (Phase 2)

The copper thermal test vehicle: the heat source that stands in for a chip.

- `ttv_hand_calcs.ipynb`: heat flux, neck gradient, thermocouple readings, heat-flow uncertainty, insulation loss, clamp force
- `cad/`: STEP files of the copper block, insulation box and clamp, plus the one-page machining drawing (PDF)
- `ansys/`: the steady-state thermal check, its plots and a short summary
- Sketches and photos of the design as it evolves
